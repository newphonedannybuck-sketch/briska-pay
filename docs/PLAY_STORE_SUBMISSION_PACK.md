### Full description

Briska Pay helps hourly-paid workers, tradespeople, construction workers, contractors and shift workers keep clear records of their working time and estimated gross pay.

Record Home and Away work days using separate hourly rates. Add break time, daily allowances, job uplifts and notes, and keep your calendar complete with paid holidays and unpaid days off.

Choose the pay schedule that matches your work, including weekly, fortnightly, four-weekly and calendar-monthly periods. Briska Pay organises recorded days into pay periods, shows the next payday and provides clear summaries of hours, estimated wages, allowances and uplifts.

Use the calendar and reports to review individual days or a whole pay period. Create Excel, PDF or CSV files when you need a timesheet-style record, then open, share, print, rename or delete supported files from the Export Library.

Your core work and pay records are stored locally on your device. Briska Pay does not require an account or cloud sync. You can create and restore manual Briska Pay backup files when you choose. Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for protected app data.

An optional app lock supports device authentication or a separate Briska Pay PIN.

Briska Pay displays one Google AdMob banner on the Home screen when Google's consent and privacy state permits advertising. It does not use interstitial, rewarded, native or app-open ads.

Briska Pay provides estimates from the information you enter. Always compare results with your official payslip and employment records. Briska Pay is not payroll, tax or accounting software and does not provide financial advice.

### Listing guardrails

- Do not call Briska Pay payroll, tax, accounting, banking or investment software.
- Do not imply that estimates are official wage calculations.
- Do not claim cloud sync, automatic backup, tax calculation or money movement.
- Do not claim that the app is ad-free, has no tracking or never uses internet access.
- Do not use rankings, awards, pricing promotions or unsupported superlatives.

## 8. Data Safety fact sheet

This is a repository fact sheet, not a completed legal or policy classification. The owner remains responsible for the final Play answers and must review the current Data Safety definitions and the current Google Mobile Ads SDK disclosure immediately before submission.

### A. Data Briska Pay stores locally

Core application data stored on the device includes:

- work/day records and dates;
- start, finish and break times;
- work type, including Home, Away, Holiday and Unpaid Day Off;
- hourly rates, allowances, uplifts and estimated gross-pay totals;
- employer/company and site names entered by the user;
- notes entered by the user;
- pay-frequency, pay-period and payday settings;
- display, currency, theme and other app preferences;
- Export Library metadata;
- optional app-lock configuration.

Security/storage facts:

- Briska Pay has no account or Briska cloud-sync backend.
- Protected Android app data is excluded from automatic cloud backup and device transfer.
- The optional PIN is protected using platform secure storage.
- Device authentication is handled by Android; Briska Pay does not receive or store fingerprint or face data.
- Core local records are not intentionally sent to AdMob as advertising content.

Current Play guidance says data accessed only on-device and not transmitted off-device may not be treated as “collected,” but the owner must apply the live form's definitions.

### B. Data Google Mobile Ads/UMP may process

The app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Current Google Mobile Ads disclosure guidance says the SDK may automatically collect and share:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general/approximate location;
- user product interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad interactions;
- diagnostic information about app/SDK performance;
- device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, app-set ID and applicable device identifiers.

Google states these data are used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention and are encrypted in transit. Actual handling can vary with SDK version, device, region, consent state, limited-ads behaviour and configuration.

UMP may process the information needed to request, present and retain advertising privacy choices. Verify its current disclosure requirements alongside the Mobile Ads SDK before completing the live form.

Mandatory live check:

- Review the Google Mobile Ads data-disclosure page for the exact SDK generation/version in the release.
- Review Play SDK Index guidance.
- Confirm collected/shared status, required/optional status, purposes and any regional/consent effects in the live form.
- Do not answer “no data collected” solely because Briska Pay has no server.

### C. User-initiated sharing

Information can leave Briska Pay when the user deliberately:

- creates, saves, opens, shares or prints an Excel, PDF or CSV export;
- creates or shares a manual backup;
- selects and restores a backup;
- sends a support email through another app.

The user chooses the destination. Once data is passed to another app, service, person or storage destination, that recipient's practices apply. Confirm how current Play definitions treat user-initiated transfers and exemptions before answering the form.

### D. Data Briska Pay does not collect through its own server

Because there is no Briska account, cloud backend or separate analytics service, Briska Pay does not collect through a Briska-operated server:

- account credentials;
- cloud copies of work/pay records;
- employer, site, note, wage or allowance records;
- exported wage files;
- contacts, messages, photos, videos or audio;
- precise GPS location;
- payment-card, bank-account or transaction information;
- health, fitness or government-identification data;
- a separate first-party analytics event stream.

This does not remove the need to disclose applicable Google Mobile Ads/UMP SDK practices.
