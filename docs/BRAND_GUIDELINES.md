# Briska Pay Brand Guidelines

This document is the single source of truth for Briska Pay branding.

## Identity

- Product name: **Briska Pay**
- Developer: **Berkzerker**
- Tagline: **Work. Track. Get Paid.**
- Use “Briska Pay” exactly as written. Do not abbreviate it to initials in product copy.

## Logo usage

Use `assets/branding/briska-bp-master.png` as the master mark. All production variants must preserve its exact BP proportions, overlap, relative positioning, colours, and polished 3D finish. Use `briska-bp-mark-transparent.png` where transparency is required and the dedicated in-app, splash, and store assets for their named contexts.

Maintain clear space equal to one quarter of the icon width on every side. Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, add effects, redraw, or reinterpret the mark. Use the approved dark black/deep-green background where a containing tile is appropriate.

## Colours

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Primary green | `#1B8F5A` |
| Secondary green / success | `#2E9D69` |
| Light background | `#F7F8FA` |
| Dark background | `#121417` |
| Primary text | `#1D1F23` |
| Secondary text | `#69707A` |
| Divider | `#E7E8EB` |
| Warning | Material Orange |
| Error | Material Red |

Do not introduce additional brand colours. Tints may use these colours with opacity.

## Typography

Poppins is the only brand typeface. Use Bold for display and headlines, SemiBold for titles and controls, Medium for subtitles, Regular for body copy, and Medium or SemiBold for captions. Keep headings concise and use sentence case.

## Icon spacing

Launcher artwork must remain inside the Android adaptive-icon safe zone. In product controls, use consistent 24 px icons with at least 12 px between icon and label and a minimum 48 px touch target.

## Cards

Cards use the theme surface, a 20 px corner radius, restrained elevation, and dividers in `#E7E8EB` on light surfaces. Content padding should normally be 18–20 px.

## Buttons

Primary buttons use `#1B8F5A`, white text, Poppins SemiBold, a minimum height of 52 px, and the shared large corner radius. Secondary actions use an outline derived from primary green. Destructive actions use Material Red.

## Tone of voice

Be professional, friendly, clear, and concise. Explain the result in human terms: “Your timesheet has been exported.” Avoid robotic status language, jargon, blame, and unnecessary exclamation marks.

## Naming conventions

Brand assets use lowercase kebab-case and begin with `briska-`. Platform icons retain platform-required resource names but must derive from the approved Briska Pay master icon. Store metadata must name Briska Pay, Berkzerker, and the official tagline consistently.
