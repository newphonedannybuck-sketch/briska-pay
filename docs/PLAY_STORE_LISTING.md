## Full Description
Briska Pay helps you track work hours, gross pay, allowances, holidays and timesheets in one privacy-focused app.

Record Home and Away work days with separate hourly rates, daily allowance, job uplift, break time and notes. Add paid holidays and unpaid days off so your records stay complete across each pay period.

Review weekly, fortnightly, four-weekly, monthly and last-Friday pay summaries. Use reports, calendar views and export tools to check your hours and gross-pay totals before comparing them with payslips or employment records.

Create exports when you need a timesheet-style record, and create manual local backup files when moving data between installs or devices. Saving, opening, printing, sharing and restoring files are actions you control.

Briska Pay stores its core work data locally on your device and does not require an account or cloud sync. Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for protected app data.

Briska Pay uses Google AdMob banner advertising on the Home screen. Where applicable, Google presents advertising privacy choices through its User Messaging Platform, and Briska Pay requests ads only when Google's consent/privacy state permits them. Advertising privacy choices can be reviewed from Settings when Google requires that option. Briska Pay does not use a separate app analytics service.

Light and dark themes are included.

Briska Pay is a personal tracking tool. It is not payroll, tax, accounting or legal advice.
