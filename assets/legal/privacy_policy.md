# Briska Pay Privacy Policy

Briska Pay is a personal hours and gross-pay tracking tool by Berkzerker.

## Local Briska Pay data

Core work/pay information is stored locally on your device. This includes work/day records, pay settings, employer or company information, app preferences, export-library metadata and optional app-lock configuration. Briska Pay does not require an account and does not operate a cloud account or cloud-sync service.

Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for Briska Pay's protected app data. Manual exports and backup files are created only when you request them. Information can leave Briska Pay when you choose to share, save, open or print an export, create or share a backup, or select and restore a backup. Other apps and destinations handle those files under their own privacy practices.

## Advertising and privacy choices

Briska Pay uses Google AdMob to show banner advertising on the Home screen. It does not use interstitial, rewarded, app-open or native ads. Google and its advertising technologies may process information needed to provide, measure, secure and manage advertising. Briska Pay does not intentionally send your wage/work records, employer name, site or job entries, or exported wage files to AdMob as advertising content.

Where applicable, Briska Pay uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google may present advertising privacy choices, and ads are requested only when Google's authoritative consent/privacy state permits them. When Google requires the option, you can review or change advertising privacy choices from Briska Pay Settings. Declining personalised advertising does not necessarily stop all advertising; Google may still permit non-personalised or limited advertising.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Google's explanation of information used by sites or apps using its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Briska Pay does not use a separate app analytics service. The Android app uses internet access for advertising and privacy services, but connectivity is not required to view or manage core wage records already stored locally.

## App lock

The optional app lock supports device authentication or a separate Briska Pay PIN. Fingerprint, face and device-credential checks are handled by the operating system. Briska Pay does not receive or store biometric data.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you edit or delete them using available app controls, reset local data, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Briska Pay cannot remotely delete local records because it does not operate a user-account server. Advertising-related information controlled by Google is separate from Briska Pay's local application records.

Diagnostic summaries are designed to exclude names, company names, pay rates, wages, allowances, notes and work-entry content.

Briska Pay does not sell your personal information.
