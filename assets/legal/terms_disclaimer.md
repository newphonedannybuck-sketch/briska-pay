# Briska Pay Terms and Disclaimer

Briska Pay is a personal hours and gross-pay tracking tool.

Calculations depend on the information entered by the user, including hours, rates, allowances, uplifts, holidays and unpaid days.

Briska Pay is not payroll, tax, accounting or legal advice. Users should verify figures against official payslips, employment records and any relevant employer guidance.

Backups and exports are controlled by the user. Keep backup files somewhere safe if you rely on them for moving data between app installs or devices.
